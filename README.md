## 📌 Task 1: Iris Dataset Exploration & Visualization

### 🎯 Objective
To explore and visualize the Iris dataset to extract meaningful insights about species characteristics.

### 📈 Key Insights
- Petal length and width show a strong positive correlation ($r \approx 0.96$).
- Setosa species is clearly separable from Versicolor and Virginica.
- Petal measurements are the most effective features for classification.

## 📌 Task 2: Credit Risk Prediction

### 🎯 Objective
Predict whether a loan applicant is likely to default on a loan based on historical data.

### 📊 Key Steps
- **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values in credit history, income, and other features.
- **EDA:** Visualized relationship between Education and Loan Status.
- **Modeling:** Trained Logistic Regression model for classification.
- **Evaluation:** Used Confusion Matrix and Accuracy Score.

---

## 📌 Task 3: Customer Churn Prediction (Bank Customers)

### 🎯 Objective
Identify bank customers at high risk of leaving the institution.

### 📊 Key Steps
- **Preprocessing:** One-Hot Encoding for categorical variables (Geography, Gender).
- **Modeling:** Random Forest Classifier achieved ~86% accuracy.
- **Insight:** Age and Balance were the most important factors influencing churn.

---

## 📌 Task 4: Predicting Insurance Claim Amounts

### 🎯 Objective
Develop a regression model to estimate medical insurance charges.

### 📊 Key Steps
- **Modeling:** Implemented Linear Regression.
- **Visualization:** Smoking status and BMI strongly impact charges.
- **Evaluation Metrics:**
  - MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
  - RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)

---

## 📌 Task 5: Personal Loan Acceptance Prediction

### 🎯 Objective
Predict which customers are likely to accept a personal loan offer from a marketing campaign.

### 📊 Key Steps
- **EDA:** Analyzed customer demographics vs acceptance rate.
- **Modeling:** Logistic Regression for classification.
- **Business Insight:** Customers with previous "Success" campaign outcomes are more likely to accept offers.
